fiction books

Fiction Books are stories created from the imagination of the author. These include novels, short stories, fantasy, mystery, and adventure books. Fiction books are meant for entertainment and sometimes to teach moral lessons. They allow readers to explore new worlds, characters, and ideas beyond real life.

Academic books

Academic books are scholarly texts written for students and researchers. They provide reliable, subject-specific knowledge, follow a formal style, and are widely used in schools, colleges, and universities to support learning, teaching, and research through structured content and verified information.

❤️

Magazines and Journals

Magazines and Journals are periodicals published weekly, monthly, or quarterly. Magazines usually contain articles, stories, and pictures on various topics like news, entertainment, fashion, or hobbies. Journals are more academic and contain research papers and articles for scholars and professionals.

❤️
❤️

TECHNICAL BOOKS

Technical Books provide detailed information on specialized subjects like engineering, programming, medicine, and information technology. These books are used by students and professionals to learn specific skills and stay updated with the latest knowledge in their fields.

POETRY BOOKS

Poetry books are collections of poems that express emotions, thoughts, and experiences through rhythmic language, imagery, and literary devices. They reflect personal feelings, culture, and imagination, allowing readers to connect deeply with human emotions and enjoy creative expression in artistic form.
